CHCH Bus Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 (app version 0.49.8)

This policy covers the CHCH Bus Tracker Android app (package nz.co.bustracker), developed by Mike Campbell, an independent developer based in Christchurch, New Zealand. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the developer.

In short: CHCH Bus Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no location tracking. Your settings stay on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporting. We do not run a server that receives or stores anything about you, and we do not sell or share any data with anyone.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your phone, in its own private storage:

  • your chosen bus stops, the routes you want shown, and your walk time settings
  • which of your stops you last viewed
  • a cached copy of the public bus timetable, so the app does not download it every time
  • whether you have dismissed a holiday timetable notice

None of this is uploaded anywhere. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Location

The app does not request or use your device location and has no location permission. The stops it shows are the ones you choose yourself.

Network use

The app connects to one service only: MetroInfo, Environment Canterbury's public transport data service, to fetch bus timetables, live departure times, vehicle positions and service alerts. Departure requests include the stop code you chose. As with any internet request, MetroInfo can see your device's IP address and basic technical details at the time of the request, and handles that under its own policies. The app does not send MetroInfo your settings, your identity or any personal information, and we do not receive any of this data.

The app's fonts and code libraries are built into the app itself, so it does not contact any other server, including advertising, analytics or font services.

Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission: internet access, which it needs to fetch departure times.

If you email us

The "Send feedback" option opens your own email app with a pre-filled message containing the app version and a line of technical device information (your phone's Android and browser version, and usually its model), so we can make sense of bug reports. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and you can edit or delete any of it first. If you do email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to improve the app. We do not add you to any mailing list, and you can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Children

CHCH Bus Tracker is a general purpose transport tool and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see or delete that correspondence under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 by contacting us at the address below.
