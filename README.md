# CHCH Bus Tracker
Know whether to stroll or sprint. Live bus departures for Christchurch stops.


CHCH Bus Tracker shows live departures for the stops you actually use, and tells you at a glance whether you can stroll or need to run.

Set your home stop and one other stop, pick the routes you care about, and the app does the rest. No account, no sign-up, no clutter.

COLOUR-CODED COUNTDOWNS
Every departure is colour-coded against your own walking time, which you set yourself:
- Easy Stroll: you have time
- Sprint or Regret: it's going to be close
- Too Late: wait for the next one
- No Rush: more than half an hour away

LIVE BUS TRACKING
Tap any departure to see where the bus actually is right now: the stop it's at or heading to, the stop it just left, and how many stops away it is from you. The bus's fleet number is shown on the card so you can tell at a glance whether the bus pulling in is yours.

BUILT FOR A REAL COMMUTE
- Two saved locations, Home and Away, one tap apart
- Show several routes at the same stop, not just one
- Search for stops by name or by stop number
- Updates every 30 seconds
- Warns you when a holiday timetable is running
- Tells you when live tracking is unavailable, rather than pretending times are real

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No advertising. No tracking. No location permission. Your stops and settings are saved on your phone and never uploaded. The app asks for one permission: internet access, so it can fetch departure times.

Real-time and timetable information is sourced from Environment Canterbury's public transport open data, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
